About DSAC

Our Leadership, Vision & Values

About Trust

Founded in the year 1994, by Shri.A.Srinivasan, the trust has been in the forefront of providing quality education at affordable cost in the region of Perambalur. The trust founded out of an immense passion for contributing to the society, strives to promote the cause of education.

In pursuance with the vision of the Founder Chairman, the trust today runs 19 Institutions, Technical & Non-Technical, in and round Perambalur catering to the educational quest of the students. These institutions are corner stone's of affordable quality education.

Dhanalakshmi Srinivasan Educational Institutions, aim to mould the student as not only a professional success but also as a true human being. We aim to be the torch bearers of the future with quality courseware, dissemination of knowledge, virtue and morality.

In this Knowledge Era, where brainpower reigns supreme over all other strengths, we aim to mould aspirants to take on, in the right path. We provide the needed academic ambience and the necessary life-skills, so that our alumni experience success beyond their chosen profession.

Chancellor Desk

Shri. A. Srinivasan, Esteemed Chancellor

Society is long dependent on education to provide the necessary stepping stone on one's path towards individual growth, which contributes directly to the growth of a society and country as a whole. In view of the ever-increasing demand for professionally qualified youth, it has become imperative to increase the availability of quality higher education in diverse fields.

Dhanalakshmi Srinivasan Agriculture College strives to establish itself as a citadel of quality education in the global arena of agriculture education. The college actively updates itself with foresight, vision and perspective of committed learning and training to meet the global demands for professional talents of international standards.

"Towards Excellence" is the motto of the institution. The institution provides world class infrastructural facilities comparable with the best of its kind in the field of education, state of the art laboratories and innovative teaching-learning process are creating the students into high technical expertise.

Principal Desk

Dr. R. Arulmozhiyan, B.Sc. (Ag.), M.Sc. (Hort.), Ph.D.

63844 50133 | deandsca@tnau.ac.in

Dr. R. Arulmozhiyan served in Tamil Nadu Agricultural University in various capacities as a faculty for 37 years in the constituent colleges like Anbil Dharmalingam Agricultural College and Research Institute, Tiruchirappalli, Horticultural College and Research Institute, Coimbatore, Horticultural College and Research Institute for Women, Tiruchirappalli and Agricultural College and Research Institute, Killikulam and as Dean at RVS Padmavathy College of Horticulture, Sempatti and triggered various technical and administrative activities.

Teaching Excellence

Involved in teaching UG students of B.Sc. (Ag) and B.Tech. (Agrl. Engg.), B.Sc. (Hort.), B.Tech. (Hort.), and further PG students pursuing Master's in Horticultural programmes through blackboard and AV modes. As a teaching professional, accomplishment has been made in the publication of hands-on manuals, books and resource materials for the benefit of the student community. Also, involved in developing Course manuals on production technology, landscaping and post-harvest technology of horticultural crops.

Research Contributions

As a researcher, worked in the various research institutes like National Pulses Research Centre, Vamban; Coconut nursery cum Botanic Gardens, Coimbatore; Sugarcane Research Station, Sirugamani and Headed Horticultural Research Station, Yercaud; authored three varieties as well as six technical bulletins. Eleven externally funded schemes were successfully completed along with 21 University research sub-projects and guided 24 students.

Published 47 national/international articles in NAAS rated journals along with 51 abstracts, 120 popular articles and 30 technical bulletins. Participated in 2 international conferences, 12 national workshops and 6 national seminars and obtained recognizing awards. Further, 5 books and 9 booklets on Horticultural crop production strategies was printed out for research reference.

A significant contribution was made in developing a wilt tolerant multi-lateraled cum bushy betel vine varieties SGM 1 and SGM (BV) 2 for the chewers and TRY (Pg) 1 Guava for the fruit growers. Organic interventions were made to enhance the production and yield of betel vine in Tamil Nadu. A new technique on cashew nut germination using 10 percent of Sodium hydroxide for high establishment in tree nurseries was developed.

Extension & Outreach

As an extension personnel, spot recommendations on farm advisories to horticultural farmers was given through 50 radio talks via AIR, Trichy and Tirunelveli as well live shows perpetuated through television media at Chennai and Coimbatore integrating media based transfer of technology.

Vision

To become a center of excellence in agricultural education, research and extension activities to serve the farming community and contribute to sustainable agricultural development.

Mission

To provide quality education in agriculture and allied sciences with state-of-the-art facilities, dedicated faculty, and industry partnerships to create skilled agricultural professionals.

Quality Policy

Our college is committed to achieve recognition as 'institution of excellence' by consistently providing quality education in the field of agriculture with professionalism and global outlook ensuring continual improvement.
