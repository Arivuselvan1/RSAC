Program Offered

B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture

4-Year Undergraduate Program

Our comprehensive B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture program is designed to provide students with in-depth knowledge and practical skills in modern agricultural practices, sustainable farming, crop management, and agricultural technology.

Program Features
  • Affiliated to Tamil Nadu Agricultural University, Coimbatore
  • 4-year degree program under credit-based semester system
  • 114 acres of cultivable land for practical training
  • State-of-the-art laboratories and research facilities
  • Experienced and dedicated faculty
  • Comprehensive hostel and campus facilities
