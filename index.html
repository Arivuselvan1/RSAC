Welcome to DS Agri

Dhanalakshmi Srinivasan Agriculture College was established in the year 2017 under the Dhanalakshmi Srinivasan Charitable and Educational Trust. The campus is having well established Farm and Dairy. The college is located 2.0 KM away from Perambalur and 75 KM North of Tiruchirapalli Airport.
